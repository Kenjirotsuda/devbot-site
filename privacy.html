Privacy Policy

How DEV BOT handles information while providing its services.

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features being used, DEV BOT may process limited Discord-related information.

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord role IDs
  • Command usage information
  • Information provided through commands

2. Why Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Provide bot functionality.
  • Perform moderation actions.
  • Identify users when required by commands.
  • Maintain server configurations.
  • Support developer and testing features.
  • Diagnose technical problems.

3. Data Storage

Some information may be temporarily processed or stored depending on the features implemented by DEV BOT.

Only information reasonably necessary for the operation of DEV BOT should be stored.

4. Third-Party Services

DEV BOT operates through Discord and may use other third-party services to provide certain functionality.

Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Requests

If you believe DEV BOT has stored information associated with you and would like it removed, contact the DEV TESTER staff through the official Discord server.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information used by DEV BOT.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as DEV BOT develops.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the DEV TESTER staff through the official Discord server.
